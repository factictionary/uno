How to play

Pick it up in 30 seconds.

  1. Match the top discard card. Play a card of the same Color or Number from your hand.
  2. Play action cards to flip the tide. Reverse, Skip, Draw 2, Wild Color Changers, and Crazy 8s change everything.
  3. Tap "LAST CARD" when you have 1 left. Forget it and you'll be punished with a Draw-2 penalty. Don't blink.
  4. Be the first to empty your hand. That's it — match, play, call LAST CARD, win.
